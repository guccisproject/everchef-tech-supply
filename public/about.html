Our Story

Quality, Without the Noise

Everchef Tech Supply was founded in East McKeesport, Pennsylvania, on a simple belief: the tools you use every day deserve the same care as anything else in your home.

We provide reliable, high-performance home electronics and modern kitchen hardware built for everyday convenience. Rather than an endless catalog, we offer a focused, carefully selected range — each piece chosen to bring quality, efficiency and sleek design into modern living spaces.
