Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Information We Collect

  • Order information: name, shipping and billing address, email, phone number and order details, collected when you check out.
  • Payment information: processed directly by Stripe, Inc. We do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Communications: information you provide when you contact us, such as your name, email, order number and message.
  • Technical information: basic data such as browser type, device and pages visited, collected by our hosting provider in server logs for security and performance.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process, fulfill, ship and support your orders, returns and warranty claims;
  • To respond to your questions and requests;
  • To detect and prevent fraud and protect the security of our website;
  • To comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations; and
  • With your consent, to improve our store through optional analytics.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Sharing Your Information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate our business — such as our payment processor (Stripe), shipping carriers, email and hosting providers — and only as needed to perform those services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights, customers or others.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential browser storage to remember the contents of your cart and your cookie preferences. These are necessary for the site to function and do not track you across other websites. Optional analytics or marketing cookies are only used if you select "Accept All" in our cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time using the link in the footer. Stripe may set its own cookies on its checkout page for fraud prevention.

Data Retention

We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfill orders, provide support and warranty service, and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements, typically up to seven years. Contact messages are retained only as long as needed to resolve your inquiry.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and a PCI-compliant payment processor, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices & Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions, by contacting us at vantelia.home@outlook.com. Depending on your state of residence, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
