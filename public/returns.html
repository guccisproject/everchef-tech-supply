Returns & Refunds

We want you to love what you bring home. If something isn't right, here's how we'll make it right.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Our 30-Day Return Policy

You may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the merchandise price. To be eligible, items must be:

  • Unused and in the same condition you received them;
  • In the original packaging with all accessories, manuals, parts and inserts included; and
  • Accompanied by your order number or proof of purchase.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Appliances that have been used for food or beverage preparation, unless defective;
  • Knives and cutlery that have been used or removed from protective packaging, unless defective;
  • Items damaged through misuse, improper installation, or failure to follow the manufacturer's instructions;
  • Gift cards; and
  • Items marked "Final Sale" at the time of purchase.

How to Start a Return

  1. Email everchef.tech@outlook.com or use our contact form with your order number, the item(s) you wish to return and the reason for the return.
  2. We'll reply within one business day with a Return Authorization (RA) number and return instructions.
  3. Pack the item securely in its original packaging, include your RA number, and ship it to the address we provide within 14 days of receiving your RA.

Returns sent without an RA number may be delayed or refused.

Return Shipping Costs

For returns due to a change of mind, the customer is responsible for return shipping costs, and original shipping charges are non-refundable. If your order qualified for free shipping, the original shipping cost will not be deducted. We recommend using a trackable shipping service; we cannot be responsible for returns lost in transit.

If we made an error, or your item arrived damaged or defective, we will provide a prepaid return label and cover all shipping costs.

Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Items

Please inspect your order upon arrival. If an item is damaged in transit, defective, or not what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos of the item and packaging. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund, including shipping, at your choice.

Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected (usually within 3 business days of arrival), we'll email you to confirm approval. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days; the time it takes to appear on your statement depends on your card issuer or bank.

Items returned incomplete, used, or damaged through no fault of ours may be refused or receive a partial refund reflecting their condition. We will contact you before issuing any partial refund.

Bundles & Sets

Bundles are priced as a set. You may return a complete bundle for a full refund. If you return only part of a bundle, the refund will equal the bundle price minus the current individual retail price of the items you keep (never less than zero).

Exchanges

The fastest way to exchange an item is to return the original for a refund and place a new order. If you'd prefer a direct exchange for the same item (for example, a replacement for a defective unit), contact us and we'll be glad to help.

Gifts

If the item was marked as a gift and shipped directly to you, you'll receive a refund to the original payment method of the purchaser, and we can notify the purchaser of your return upon request.

Limited Warranty

Electrical appliances and electronics purchased from Everchef Tech Supply are covered by a one-year limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship under normal household use, beginning on the date of delivery. If a covered defect appears after the 30-day return window, contact us with your order number, a description of the issue and photos or video where possible. We will, at our option, repair or replace the product, or refund the purchase price if repair or replacement is not reasonably available. This warranty does not cover normal wear, cosmetic damage, misuse, accidents, commercial use, unauthorized repairs or modifications. Some products may also carry a separate manufacturer's warranty. Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under applicable consumer protection law.
