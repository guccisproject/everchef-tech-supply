Shipping Policy

How and when your order reaches you.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days (Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays) after payment is confirmed. Orders placed after 2:00 PM ET, or on weekends and holidays, begin processing the next business day. During peak seasons and promotional events, processing may take up to 3 business days.

You will receive an order confirmation email immediately after purchase and a shipping confirmation with tracking information once your order leaves our facility.

Transit times are estimates provided by our carriers and begin once your order ships. They are not guaranteed and may be affected by weather, carrier delays or other events outside our control. The free shipping threshold applies to the merchandise subtotal after discounts and before taxes.

Where We Ship

We currently ship to addresses within the United States, including Alaska, Hawaii, APO/FPO/DPO addresses and U.S. territories. Deliveries to Alaska, Hawaii, territories and military addresses may require additional transit time, and expedited service may not be available to every location. We do not currently ship internationally.

Due to size and weight, some larger appliances and bundles cannot be delivered to P.O. boxes. If an order cannot be delivered to the address provided, we will contact you to arrange an alternative.

Sales Tax

Applicable state and local sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your shipping address.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email containing your tracking number and a link to follow your package. Please allow up to 24 hours for tracking information to update. If you have not received a shipping confirmation within 3 business days of placing your order, please contact us.

Bundles & Multi-Item Orders

Bundles and sets are packed to ship together whenever possible. Occasionally, multi-item orders may arrive in separate packages; your shipping confirmation will include tracking for each.

Address Accuracy

Please review your shipping address carefully at checkout. We are not responsible for orders delivered to an incorrect address provided by the customer. If you notice an error, contact us as soon as possible — we can update the address only if the order has not yet shipped. Packages returned to us as undeliverable may be reshipped at the customer's expense or refunded less original shipping charges.

Lost, Stolen or Damaged Packages

Risk of loss passes to you upon delivery to the carrier's confirmed delivery location. If your tracking shows a package as delivered but you cannot locate it, please check with neighbors and household members and contact the carrier, then reach out to us within 7 days of the delivery date so we can help open a claim.

If your order arrives damaged, please photograph the packaging and the item and contact us within 7 days of delivery. We will arrange a replacement or refund at no cost to you. See our Returns & Refunds Policy for details.
